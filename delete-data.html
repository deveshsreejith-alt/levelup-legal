Delete your Level Up data

This page explains how to delete data for Level Up: Fitness RPG (package app.levelup.fitness), published on Google Play by Dev looper.

Where your data is stored

Level Up has no user accounts. Your progress, workouts, runs, meal and calorie logs, profile details and downloaded AI model files are stored only on your device. We do not keep a copy on our servers. Meal-scan photos are analyzed on your device and are not saved.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Level Up and open Settings.
  2. Scroll to Privacy & your data.
  3. Tap Delete all local data and confirm.

This permanently erases your progress, logs and downloaded model files from the phone. You can also clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Level Up → Storage, or uninstall the app.

Option 2: Email us

Email togi+levelup@mail.grokbot.com with the subject "Level Up data deletion". Because your data is on your device, we will help you remove it and will delete any messages or related information you have sent us. We respond within 30 days.

Purchases

Premium purchase records are kept by Google Play and our purchase-verification provider RevenueCat (linked only to a random anonymous ID) as required for billing and legal purposes. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription; manage or cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
