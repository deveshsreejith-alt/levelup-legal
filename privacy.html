Level Up Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026
Developer: Dev looper
Contact: togi+levelup@mail.grokbot.com

Level Up is a fitness and activity app for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored and who receives it. In short: your workout, nutrition and profile data stay on your device. Level Up has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no developer server.

1. Information stored on your device

Level Up saves the following only in local storage on your phone (an on-device SQLite database):

  • Profile name and fitness goal you enter
  • Workout history, exercise, set and rest durations, streaks, rest days, XP, avatar cosmetics and loot rewards
  • Estimated walking/running distance from active GPS workouts
  • Body weight (used to estimate workout calories burned)
  • The daily total calories you choose to type in
  • Meal records you enter (names and calorie totals)

You do not need to create an account. We (the developer) cannot see this data, because it is not uploaded to us.

2. Camera and meal-photo calorie estimates (Premium)

The camera is used only when you tap Scan a meal and grant camera permission. The photo is analyzed on your device by an AI image model to show an estimated calorie count. The photo is not sent to us or to any AI service, and the scan result is not saved to your log. Photo-library access is not requested.

Calorie estimates are rough approximations, not measurements or medical/dietary advice.

3. On-device AI model download

The first time you scan a meal, the app downloads about 550 MB of model files (SmolVLM-500M) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and its content-delivery providers. Like any web download, those servers receive your IP address and standard request information. Your photo is never part of that download request. The model files are stored in the app's private storage.

4. Location

When you start a walking or running workout, the app asks for location permission while the app is in use to estimate distance. Background location is not requested. Location readings are processed on the device to calculate distance; your route coordinates are not stored or sent anywhere. You can withdraw permission in Android settings at any time.

5. Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Level Up is free to download. Optional Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment; we never receive your card details.

We use RevenueCat to verify Premium purchases. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous app-user ID, purchase/transaction details, product IDs, entitlement status and technical device/network information necessary to provide its service. The app disables RevenueCat's automatic device-identifier collection and diagnostics. Your name, weight, photos, calories and workout records are not sent to RevenueCat or Google by Level Up.

An internet connection is needed to buy, restore and re-verify Premium at least every 72 hours.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising or ad SDKs
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of personal or health data
  • No social features or public profiles
  • No developer-operated server receiving your data

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it. You can erase everything in Settings → Privacy & your data → Delete all local data (this removes your progress, logs and downloaded model files), clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Deleting local data does not cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions in Google Play. Android device backup of app data is disabled for Level Up.

Google and RevenueCat retain purchase records as needed for restoring purchases, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations. To request access or deletion of purchase data held by RevenueCat on our behalf, contact togi+levelup@mail.grokbot.com.

8. Children

Level Up is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Minors should get a parent's or guardian's permission before purchasing.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal information and to complain to a data-protection authority. Because most data never leaves your device, you control it directly; for anything else contact togi+levelup@mail.grokbot.com.
